Ask the live contract yourself

This page asks the deployed contract one question: is this vehicle registered? Your wallet proves the call, pays its fee in your own DUST, and submits it. Nothing passes through a server of ours, and the page never sees a key.

1. Your wallet

Looking for a Midnight wallet on this page…

You need a Midnight wallet that speaks the DApp Connector (1AM or Lace), set to the Preview network, holding DUST. 1AM proves in the page; Lace proves through a proof server running on your own machine.

2. The question
