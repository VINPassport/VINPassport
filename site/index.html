Why a passport

Checkable by strangers. Confidential to everyone else.

A vehicle passport has to satisfy two people who want opposite things. The buyer wants proof. The owner and the dealer want their details kept private.

Option A

Publish the full history

Buyers can check it, but owners become identifiable and dealers expose prices and commercial records.

Option B

Keep the history private

Owners stay protected, but the buyer is back to taking the seller's word for it.

VINPassport

Prove the answer, keep the record

Each answer is proven from the record without publishing the record. The buyer gets a yes; nobody gets the file.

How it works

Issue once. Update forward. Verify anywhere.

  1. 01

    Issue on the production line

    The manufacturer opens the passport as the vehicle is built, so there is no earlier history to misstate. Every entry carries the name of the issuer who wrote it.

  2. 02

    Update as the vehicle lives

    Approved registrars add services, readings and repairs. Mileage, keeper and service counts can only move forward; a lower number is refused.

  3. 03

    Verify with a scan

    A QR code on the vehicle or its listing opens plain answers in the browser. No app, no account and no fee for the buyer.

Cannot be rolled back.

Mileage, keepers, services and write-off category only ever move forward. A lower reading is not something we choose not to record; it is an update the ledger refuses.
