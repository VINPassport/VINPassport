Engine hours

Hours that can only go up.

On a boat, a jet ski or a quad, engine hours matter as much as mileage, and an hour meter can be swapped or reset. A passport records each reading from the service yard, and a lower reading is refused before it is ever written. The same rule that protects a car's mileage protects your hours.

How it would work

Started by the builder, kept up by the yard, checked by the buyer

  1. 01

    Issued at handover

    The builder or first dealer opens the passport against the vehicle's own ID, with the facts only they can vouch for.

  2. 02

    Kept up by approved hands

    Service yards, dealers and surveyors you approve add readings, repairs and inspections over the vehicle's life.

  3. 03

    Checked in seconds

    A buyer scans the passport's QR code or enters the ID, and gets plain answers. No app, no account, free.

Where we are

Voluntary, and coming next

The EU's Euro 7 passport rules apply to new cars and vans, not to boats, quads or motorcycles. So a lifestyle passport is a choice a builder or owner makes because it helps them sell, not because a regulator asks for it.
