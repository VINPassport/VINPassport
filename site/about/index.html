About

A vehicle's history should travel with it, and prove itself.

Used-car buyers across Europe still rely on the seller's word and a patchwork of national records that rarely cross borders. VINPassport gives every vehicle one record, written at the source, that anyone can check and no one can quietly rewrite.

What we hold to

Three principles

Proof, not promises

Every answer is backed by a proof anyone can check, from a writer anyone can name.

Private by design

Owners, dealers and manufacturers share answers, never their records.

Honest about limits

We publish what a passport cannot prove, alongside what it can.

Who builds it

MJ Krugell, founder

MJ Krugell works where zero-knowledge proofs meet regulation: proving exactly what a rule asks for and keeping everything else private. As a Midnight Nightforce Leader, MJ helps bring that approach to companies. VINPassport applies it to vehicle records. Waterline, built for the Midnight Korea Hackathon 2026, applies it to rental deposits, showing a safety verdict and never the amounts behind it.
