How a check works

Commit, prove, refuse.

The mechanics behind the plain answers on the buyer's screen.

  1. Store

    The ledger keeps persistentCommit(value, salt) for each field, never the value or the salt. Records are keyed by the hash of an identifier.

  2. Prove

    A check proves a value satisfies a bound, such as mileage under 150,000 km, without publishing the value. Transactions are built, proven and signed locally.

  3. Refuse

    Each field's rule is fixed at creation. Under neverFalls, a lower later reading fails in-circuit and writes nothing. Writes are bound to a named registrar, and changing who may register takes two registrars' approval.
